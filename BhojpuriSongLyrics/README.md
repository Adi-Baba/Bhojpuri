# भोजपुरी गीत-संग्रह (Bhojpuri Song Lyrics Archive)

**Bhojpuri Revive Project** — Song Lyrics Documentation Initiative

---

## उद्देश्य (Purpose)

भोजपुरी भाषा के लोक गीत, फ़िल्मी गीत, आ सांस्कृतिक काव्य के एक जगह दस्तावेज़ीकरण करना।  
To document, preserve, and translate Bhojpuri folk songs, film lyrics, and cultural poetry in one organised archive.

---

## फोल्डर संरचना (Folder Structure)

```
BhojpuriSongLyrics/
│
├── 01_Lok_Geet/          लोक गीत     — Traditional folk songs
├── 02_Birha/             बिरहा       — Songs of separation & longing
├── 03_Chaita/            चइता        — Spring season songs (Chaitra month)
├── 04_Kajri/             कजरी        — Monsoon season songs (Sawan)
├── 05_Sohar/             सोहर        — Birth celebration songs
├── 06_Vivah_Geet/        विवाह गीत   — Wedding songs
├── 07_Nautanki_Naach/    नौटंकी नाच  — Folk theatre & dance songs
├── 08_Phaguwa_Holi/      फगुआ/होली   — Holi festival songs
├── 09_Film_Songs/        फ़िल्मी गीत  — Bhojpuri cinema songs
├── 10_Devotional_Bhakti/ भक्ति गीत   — Devotional & religious songs
├── 11_Modern_Pop/        आधुनिक पॉप  — Contemporary Bhojpuri pop
├── 12_Misc_Folk/         विविध लोक   — Miscellaneous folk genres
│
├── _archive/             Older versions, drafts
├── _resources/           References, dictionaries, grammar notes
└── _contributors/        Contributor bios & attribution
```

Each genre folder contains three subfolders:
- `raw/`         — Original Bhojpuri lyrics (Devanagari)
- `translated/`  — Hindi / English translation
- `notes/`       — Singer info, region, occasion, musical notes

---

## फ़ाइल नामकरण (File Naming Convention)

```
[genre-code]_[song-title-slug]_[source].txt

Example:
  01_lok_geet_nadi_naare_na_jao_shyam_trad.txt
  09_film_songs_lahariya_luta_film1963.txt
```

---

## योगदान (Contributing)

1. सही फोल्डर में रखें।
2. `raw/` में मूल भोजपुरी देवनागरी में लिखें।
3. `notes/` में गायक, क्षेत्र, अवसर ज़रूर लिखें।
4. अगर अनुवाद हो त `translated/` में डालें।
